Chirpy scores related posts by shared tags and categories, so every post in an era would recommend every other post in that era purely because they happened at the same job. Done as an empty include rather than by forking _layouts/post.html, so there is no layout to re-reconcile on a theme upgrade. The gem's post.html still calls this include; it just gets nothing back.
