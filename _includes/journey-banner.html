{%- comment -%} Career timeline as a static SVG. Rendered entirely at build time: no client JavaScript, no chart library. Adapted from the JourneyBanner component in yizhang7210/professional-site-template (MIT). x = time, y = scope of the role, color = discipline in the fixed order declared in _data/journey.yml. Posts attach to an era through their category: a post with `categories: [Gradle]` plots as a dot on the Gradle bar at its publish date, and the bar itself links to that era's post list. A bar with no posts is left unlinked -- jekyll-archives only generates an era page once a post claims it, so linking an empty era would point at a 404. The coordinate space stays close to the rendered pixel size. A much larger viewBox gets scaled down into the content column, shrinking every font with it and leaving the labels unreadable. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign data = site.data.journey -%} {%- assign W = 800 -%} {%- assign H = 420 -%} {%- assign PX = 56 -%} {%- assign INNER = 688 -%} {%- assign PT = 64 -%} {%- assign BAR_H = 20 -%} {%- assign MIN_W = 12 -%} {%- assign TIMELINE_Y = 372 -%} {%- assign CHART_H = 288 -%} {%- comment -%} Time domain: earliest start year through next year. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign startYear = 9999 -%} {%- for r in data.roles -%} {%- assign y = r.start | split: '-' | first | plus: 0 -%} {%- if y < startYear -%}{%- assign startYear = y -%}{%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- assign endYear = 'now' | date: '%Y' | plus: 1 -%} {%- assign span = endYear | minus: startYear -%} {%- assign spanF = span | times: 1.0 -%}
